The preseason is over, the roster is set, and the opener is 11 days away. It was the busiest week of the offseason — a wild finish at Lambeau, thirty players cut, a quarterback traded two days after the best game of his career, and the Micah Parsons question finally answered on paper. Here's all of it.

---

## 42-38, and the Backup Quarterback Won It

The preseason finale against Arizona turned into the most entertaining game of the three by a distance. Green Bay rested Jordan Love *and* Tyrod Taylor, handed the whole game to **Kyle McCord**, and he went 22-of-35 for 226 yards and **four touchdowns** — including two to Kisean Johnson in the last three and a half minutes to erase an 11-point deficit.

Arizona still got a Hail Mary off at the buzzer. It got batted down by a crowd of green and gold.

Matt LaFleur beat his little brother Mike in their first meeting as head coaches. Undrafted rookie receiver **J. Michael Sturdivant** picked the right night for six catches, 112 yards and a touchdown — hold that thought.

---

## Then They Traded Him

Two days after the four-touchdown game, **McCord was traded to the Miami Dolphins for a 2028 sixth-round pick.**

It reads harsh, but the logic holds up. Resting Taylor in the finale had already settled the QB2 job — the veteran was always going to be Love's backup. That left McCord as a player Green Bay would almost certainly have lost on waivers for nothing. Turning him into a draft pick was the better outcome for everyone, McCord included: he lands somewhere he might actually play.

Tyrod Taylor is QB2. McCord got a fresh start and the Packers got an asset. Fine business.

---

## Trey Smack Finished the Preseason Perfect

Two field goals against Arizona, from 36 and 37. That closes the book on a preseason where the rookie went **9-for-9**, with three makes from 50-plus and a long of 59.

Three weeks ago he was 8-for-13 in camp and his job looked genuinely uncertain. He is now the least worrying thing about this football team.

---

## Cutdown Day: 30 Players Released

Green Bay got to 53 on Sunday. Per Packers.com, they kept **six receivers and seven defensive linemen** — an aggressive split that cost some well-regarded players their spots.

**The receiver room:** Christian Watson, Matthew Golden, Jayden Reed, Bo Melton, Skyy Moore, and — the genuine surprise — **undrafted rookie J. Michael Sturdivant**, who was a standout all summer and closed with that 112-yard night. Released: Will Sheppard, Isaiah Neyor, Kaden Prather, Chris Hilton, and **Kisean Johnson**, who caught two touchdowns in the final four minutes of the preseason and got cut two days later. That's the brutal arithmetic of the 53.

**Savion Williams** landed on injured reserve with an ankle injury and will miss at least four games — a real blow for a receiver who was in line for a rotational role and return duties.

Also to reserve lists: **Luke Musgrave** (neck) and **Jordon Riley** on reserve/PUP, **Collin Oliver** on IR/designated for return.

---

## Micah Parsons: Officially Out Until Week 5

The move everyone expected became official. Parsons went on **reserve/PUP**, which by rule keeps him out for the first four games:

- Week 1 — at Minnesota
- Week 2 — at N.Y. Jets
- Week 3 — vs. Atlanta
- Week 4 — at Tampa Bay

He's eligible to return from Week 5 onward. The date circled on everyone's calendar is still **Week 6 at Lambeau against Dallas** — his former team, on Sunday Night Football. Nine months after tearing his ACL chasing Bo Nix, that would be a hell of a way to come back.

---

## Practice Squad

Green Bay lost exactly one player on waivers: defensive lineman **Nazir Stackhouse**, claimed by Tennessee. Everyone else cleared, and several came straight back — Murvin Kenion III, sixth-round pick Domani Jackson, and Pierre Strong Jr. among the early practice squad re-signings. Teams can carry 16 this year.

---

## Next Up: The Real Thing

**Week 1 — Sunday, Sept. 13, at the Minnesota Vikings. 3:25 p.m. CT / 5:25 p.m. Brasília time, on CBS.**

A road opener against a division rival, without your best pass rusher. Eleven days.
