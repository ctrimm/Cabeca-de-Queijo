Much better week. The Packers went to Denver and won going away, the rookie kicker kept doing rookie kicker things, and the roster is about to get very real — final cuts land Sunday. Oh, and the head coach's little brother is at Lambeau today. Let's go.

---

## 33-13 in Denver

Green Bay picked up its first preseason win and did it convincingly, snapping a Broncos preseason win streak that had reached eight games.

Tyrod Taylor found Will Sheppard for a 2-yard touchdown to put the Packers up 17-10, and Trey Smack's kicking sent them into halftime ahead 23-10. Jaden Nixon's 3-yard touchdown run made it 30-13 late. Bo Nix, playing his first live snaps since breaking his right ankle in the playoffs seven months ago, went 6-of-9 for 76 yards and a touchdown for Denver.

Per Sports Illustrated's recap, the Packers were the better team in all three phases. In late August, that's about all you can ask.

---

## The Kicker Question Is Closed

Two weeks ago Trey Smack was 8-for-13 in camp and the job looked shaky. Now he's **7-for-7 on field goals through two preseason games**, including four in Denver and makes from beyond 50 in both games.

The best endorsement came from the other sideline. Per A to Z Sports, Broncos head coach Sean Payton said afterward: *"The kicker is going to be the player of the game, right? The rookie."*

Nobody in Green Bay is asking who the kicker is anymore.

---

## Backup Quarterback Just Became a Real Decision

This one is worth watching into the weekend. **Kyle McCord** — a 23-year-old who was a sixth-round pick in Philadelphia last year and signed a futures deal with Green Bay after a season on the Eagles' practice squad — has outplayed the veteran.

Through the preseason, per Acme Packing Company and 247Sports, McCord is 12-of-16 for 102 yards with a 91.1 passer rating. **Tyrod Taylor**, the 16-year veteran signed specifically to back up Jordan Love, is 4-of-8 for 21 yards with a 56.3 rating.

Experience still counts for a lot in a QB2, and Taylor was signed for exactly that. But this is no longer a formality, and Brian Gutekunst has to decide by Sunday.

---

## Today: LaFleur vs. LaFleur

The Cardinals arrived in Green Bay for a joint practice at Ray Nitschke Field — **today at 11:30 a.m. CT**, per Packers.com, and it's the 13th and final public practice of training camp.

The subplot writes itself: Arizona is coached by **Mike LaFleur**, 39, in the first year of his first head coaching job. Green Bay is coached by his older brother **Matt**, 46, now in his eighth season. They meet again Friday night in the preseason finale.

The Packers are also running the bike tradition for the occasion — kids of any fan affiliation are invited to bring bikes to Lambeau for players to ride to practice, Cardinals included.

**Preseason finale: Friday, Aug. 28 — 7 p.m. CT / 9 p.m. Brasília time, at Lambeau Field.**

---

## Injury Update

Per Acme Packing Company and Cheesehead TV:

- **Josh Jacobs (groin)** — back at practice for the first time since Aug. 6, limited in team periods. Trending the right way.
- **Aaron Banks (knee)** — took 11-on-11 snaps and had his reps managed. LaFleur says he's on pace for Week 1.
- **Tucker Kraft (knee)** — still in 11-on-11 work after last week's return.
- **Brandon Cisse** — back from injury, and immediately stuffed Jacobs in a run drill. The rookie corner keeps making a case.
- **Micah Parsons (ACL)** — no change. Mid-October remains the expectation, with Week 5 or 6 the optimistic window.

---

## Cuts Are Coming — and Earlier Than Usual

Heads up if you're used to the old calendar: the NFL moved the deadline up this year. Teams must be down from 90 to **53 players by Sunday, Aug. 30 at 6 p.m. ET — 7 p.m. Brasília time** — two days earlier than the Tuesday deadline of recent seasons. Waiver claims are due Monday at 1 p.m. ET.

So Friday's game against Arizona is the last audition for a lot of guys, and the bubble is crowded — receiver, running back depth, and the back end of the secondary are all still being sorted.
